CEIT Inventory

2026 · Thesis · Live

My capstone. Every piece of equipment in the CEIT labs gets its own QR label. Staff scan it to update the record, and students scan it to borrow the item or report that it's broken. I'm the only developer on it, and it's live.

  • QR labels, one at a time or a full A4 sheet
  • Borrowing, reservations and overdue reminders
  • Repair tickets and a log of every change
  • Import from Excel or CSV, checked before it saves
  • Reports you can view or download

Next.js 16 · React 19 · Prisma · PostgreSQL · Supabase · Playwright

Para

2026 · Mobile app · Heading to Play Store & App Store

Jeepneys don't have timetables or an official map, so most people learn the routes by asking around. Para puts the routes on a map, plans the trip, and tells you the fare before you get on.

  • Every route drawn as its signboard, and it works offline
  • Trip planning on the phone, so where you're going stays private
  • Fares from the official tables, with student, senior and PWD rates
  • Driver Mode, so people waiting can see the jeep coming

Flutter · Dart · Supabase · PostGIS · MapLibre · Next.js admin panel · ~1,000 automated tests

PH Traffic Index

2026 · Data + web · Collecting since Oct 26

12 AM6 AM12 PM6 PM11 PM

One reading every hour, at minute 7Move your mouse over the hours

I wanted to know which roads are actually the worst and at what time, and I couldn't find that data anywhere, so I started collecting it. Every hour a script asks TomTom how long 25 routes take with traffic and saves the answer.

  • Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, Baguio and more, plus 6 highways
  • Rankings, rush hour curves and event comparisons
  • A counter that keeps it on TomTom's free plan
  • Runs on GitHub, Supabase and Vercel, no server to pay for

Next.js · TypeScript · Supabase · GitHub Actions · TomTom Routing API · MapLibre
